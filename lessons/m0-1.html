A notebook with ticks

Remember your school notebook? The teacher gave you one page a day. When you finished a page, you got a tick ✓. At the end of the year you could flip through and see all your ticks.

This site works the same way. One lesson a day. Finish it, tick it. The home page shows all your ticks and how far you have come.

How the steps are written

When a step says to press keys, they look like this: Ctrl + C. That means: hold down the Ctrl key (bottom-left of the keyboard), tap C once while still holding it, then let go of both.

When a step says to click something on screen, its name looks like this: Sign up. Look for a button or link with exactly those words.

The three shortcuts you will use most:

To do thisWith the mouseWith the keyboard
Copy selected textRight-click on it → CopyCtrl + C
PasteRight-click where you want it → PasteCtrl + V
Undo a mistakeUsually an ↶ arrow buttonCtrl + Z

To select text: put the mouse at the start of the text, press and hold the left mouse button, drag to the end of the text, then let go. The text turns blue. Now you can copy it.

Set the site up on your computer

A. Open the site

  1. If someone sent you the link, click it once. It opens in your browser. Done; skip to part B.
  2. If you have the link written somewhere (like a note), select the whole link (see above), then right-click on it → Copy. Or press Ctrl + C.
  3. Open Google Chrome (or Edge). Click once on the long white bar at the top (the address bar). Or press Ctrl + L.
  4. Right-click in the address bar → Paste. Or press Ctrl + V.
  5. Press Enter. The home page opens.

B. Bookmark it so you never lose it

  1. While you are on the home page, press Ctrl + D. A small box appears near the top right.
  2. Click Done. The page is now saved as a bookmark.
  3. To see your bookmarks under the address bar all the time, press Ctrl + Shift + B. Now "System Design from Zero" sits there; one click opens it.

C. Put it on your phone's home screen (optional)

Android (Chrome):

  1. Open the site link in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three dots) at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (it may say Install), then tap Add.
  4. An icon now sits on your phone's home screen, like an app.

iPhone (Safari):

  1. Open the site link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up), at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add at the top right.

D. Tick a lesson as done

  1. Scroll to the very bottom of any lesson (or press End on the keyboard).
  2. Click the big blue button Mark this lesson as done. The box turns green.
  3. Clicked by mistake? Click the same button again to remove the tick.
  4. Go back to the home page (click ← All lessons at the top left). The lesson now shows a green ✓, and your progress bar has grown.

E. Back up your ticks (do this every Sunday)

Your ticks are saved only in this browser on this device. Your phone cannot see your laptop's ticks. If you clear your browsing history, the ticks are deleted.
  1. Go to the home page. Scroll down to Your progress backup.
  2. Click Download my progress. A file called system-design-progress.json is saved in your Downloads folder.
  3. To bring your ticks back (or move them to another device): copy that file to the other device, open the home page there, click Load a progress file, click the file once, then click Open.

F. Two more buttons you will meet

  1. Hidden answers. In "Check yourself", every question is a closed box. First answer it in your head, then click the question to see the answer. Click again to close it.
  2. Copy buttons. Grey boxes with code have a small Copy button at the top right. Click it, then paste where the lesson tells you with Ctrl + V.
Try it: click Copy on this box, then paste it into the address bar.

The Theme button at the top right switches between light and dark colours. Dark is easier on the eyes at night.

Quick quiz

Where are your ticks saved?

Only in the browser you are using, on the device you are using.

You ticked 10 lessons on your laptop. Will your phone show them?

No. Download your progress on the laptop, send the file to your phone (for example by WhatsApp to yourself or by email), then click Load a progress file on the phone.

What does Ctrl + V do?

It pastes whatever you copied last.

How long should one lesson take?

About 2 hours. Taking two days for one lesson is fine.
