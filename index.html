What is system design?

Imagine you cook lunch for your family. Easy: one kitchen, one cook, four plates.

Now imagine you must deliver lunch to one lakh people, every day, on time, even when a cook is sick or a road is blocked. You need many kitchens, a way to take orders, delivery riders, a plan for when things break, and a way to remember who ordered what.

Planning all of that is system design. In software, the "lunch" is a web page, a chat message or a payment, and the "kitchens" are computers called servers.

How every lesson works
